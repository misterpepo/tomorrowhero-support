Getting started

01 / BEGIN

Set out when you're ready

Choose “Start quitting” or “Track or cut down first” during setup. Your brand and daily average can wait. Preparation mode keeps the smoke-free timer off until you choose to begin.

02 / PAUSE

A craving? Call Assist

Start a 30-second Assist from Home. The “At work” option is silent and automatic. It finishes even without input, and closing early never takes away your progress.

03 / RECORD

A diary for your daily count

Enter a daily total and confirm completed days. Timed smoking entries are included in the total, not added twice. Unrecorded days are never treated as zero.

04 / EXPLORE

Collect, read, and take your time

Collect one daily gift. Stories arrive as journey days pass. Explore regions, craft equipment, and restore the village at your own pace.

Common questions

What happens if I smoke again?

You keep your hero, equipment, bond with Pokko, and unlocked adventures. After logging smoking, you can restart immediately. There is no need to wait until tomorrow.

Why do the timer and savings need confirmation?

A smoking entry with an unknown time overlaps your current smoke-free period. On Home, set the last time you smoked or restart from now. The app does not guess a smoking time from a daily total, and your diary counts stay unchanged.

How do I correct a record?

In Journal history, correct a date, count, or brand, or cancel an entry. Correct daily totals in the cigarette diary. The correction history is kept, and corrections never remove your hero's growth.

Why aren't estimated savings shown?

Enter your usual brand's pack size, price, and currency, plus your daily average before quitting in Settings. Home shows an estimate for your current smoke-free period. Version 2.0 also lets you review totals across past periods. Different currencies are not converted or combined.

How do reminders, widgets, and sound work?

In Settings, choose a daily reminder, a weekly Monday reminder, or none. Version 2.0 also offers optional milestone and village-letter notices. Permission is requested only when you enable a notice. After setup, add the widget to your iPhone's Home Screen or Lock Screen. In version 2.0, Assist can also be launched through Shortcuts and Control Center. Music and sound effects can be changed in Settings and remain silent in Silent Mode.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to another phone?

Records are stored on your device. Only widget display information is shared with this app's widget. There is no app account or app-managed cloud sync. In version 2.0, use the backup section in Settings to export your journal and adventure progress as a JSON file, then import it on your new phone. Importing keeps existing data and adds missing records without duplicating the same file. Sound and notification preferences are not included. You choose the destination; keep the file safe because it contains smoking records. OS backups and device transfers also depend on your settings and available backups. The developer does not keep a copy of your records.

How do I delete my data?

Use “Delete App” in iPhone Settings to remove the app's on-device data. Offloading the app or removing its Home Screen icon may keep data. Manage OS backups separately through Apple's settings. Exported files remain after deleting the app; delete them separately from their saved locations.

The app won't open or save.

Reopen the app and check your iPhone's available storage. The app does not hide storage errors by deleting your data. If an action is shown as unsaved, use its retry button. Contact support before deleting the app if the problem continues.
