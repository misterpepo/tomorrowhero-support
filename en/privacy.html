Privacy Policy

Applies to Tomorrow Hero: Quit Smoking / 明日の勇者 - 禁煙RPG
Updated November 12, 2026 · Provider: Mister Develop

Information stored on your device

The app stores brands, prices, currencies, your average count before quitting, quit starts and restarts, smoking times and counts, daily totals and confirmation status, craving situations, intensity and notes, reduction targets, personal reasons and savings rewards, correction history, journey progress, rewards, equipment, and village state on your device. Audio preferences, completed Assist counts, notification settings, and the history used to decide when to request an app review also stay on your device.

No account is required. The app does not automatically send these records to a developer-operated server, and has no advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or HealthKit integration.

Sharing with the widget

The app and its widget use an App Group belonging to the same developer. It stores the current status, the start time of your current period, hero level and title, equipment name, bond label, journey days and the journey start date and time zone, update time, and a display image. The diary database itself is not shared with the widget. Information shown in a widget or on the Lock Screen may be visible to people who can see your device.

User-chosen exports and sharing (version 2.0 and later)

You can export a JSON file containing your diary, smoking and craving records, custom brands, targets and reasons, and adventure progress. Export and import happen only when you choose them, using the iOS file picker. The file includes smoking records and is not separately encrypted by the app, so choose its storage location and recipients carefully. If you choose iCloud Drive or another provider, that service's terms and privacy policy apply. The file is not sent to the developer. Sound and notification preferences are not included.

Sharing a hero picture is optional. The image includes your hero, level, title, and journey day, but not smoking records, smoke-free time, or savings. You choose the destination in the iOS share sheet, and that service's privacy policy applies. Assist through Shortcuts or Siri uses OS features without a developer-operated server.

OS backups and device transfers

There is no app-managed cloud sync. Depending on your OS and settings, data may be included in iCloud or computer backups and transfers between devices. Those features follow Apple's services and settings. The developer does not receive a copy of your backup.

Notifications

The app requests notification permission only when you enable a notice, and schedules daily reminders, milestones, and village-letter notices locally on your device. No developer-operated server is used. Reminder text does not include smoking records or smoke-free time. Reminders do not affect game rewards.

Optional support inquiries

If you contact us by email, we use your email address, message, and information you choose to send to respond and investigate problems. You do not need to send smoking records. Email service providers process the sending and receiving of email. We retain support information for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and delete information no longer needed. You may contact us below to request deletion of support correspondence.

External links and this website

Opening support, privacy, or health-information source links takes you to an external browser or email app. The destination's privacy policy applies. This website has no advertising or analytics scripts added by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process connection information. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Managing and deleting records

You can correct or cancel records in the app, with correction history retained. Use “Delete App” on your iPhone to delete on-device app data. Offloading the app or removing its Home Screen icon may keep data. Manage OS backups separately. Exported files and shared images remain after deleting the app; manage and delete them at their respective destinations. The developer cannot remotely view, restore, or delete your on-device records.

Audience and changes

The app is intended for adults and is not a medical service. If features or data handling change, we will update this page and provide additional notice in the app or elsewhere when appropriate for significant changes.
